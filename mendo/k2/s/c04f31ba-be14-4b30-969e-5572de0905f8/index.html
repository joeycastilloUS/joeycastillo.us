Executive Kaizen — Install Night 5

The night’s single biggest loss was a skipped smoke test: Gainey Ranch never ran it, so a dead POS switch hid for 1h 27m — by the time it showed offline in Meraki, it was too late — and finished crews at two sites waited on one site’s diagnosis. The fix is not a new tool; it is making the smoke test a confirmed, per-site gate — released AND executed AND reported — before anyone goes idle.

  1. Smoke test as a confirmed per-site gate (#1) — “SMOKE FREE” was broadcast at 10:14 PM; Gainey never executed. By the time the POS switch showed offline in Meraki at 11:11 PM it was too late — Gainey came online 1:51 AM, and Walnut Creek (~39m) and Pacific City (~57m) waited at standby. 01 Release is not execution — gate the smoke test per site

01 Smoke test becomes a confirmed per-site gate: released, executed, reported ★

Convert “SMOKE FREE” from a broadcast into a checklist gate: after Toast config, each site runs the smoke test and reports “SMOKE PASSED” (or FAILED) to the bridge; the operator does not go IDLE and no site proceeds to full testing until all sites have reported. Track it on the board like network flips were (WAL 9:34 / GR 9:44 / PAC 9:57 — the flip had per-site confirmation; the smoke test gets the same).

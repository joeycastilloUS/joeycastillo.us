Where the artifacts disagree

DIV-001 — which exceptions are open
The two shipped artifacts do not agree, and both say 'five'. Applying the status field literally gives EXC-002, EXC-005, EXC-008, EXC-009, EXC-010. The Open Items table in the published AAR lists EXC-002, EXC-005, EXC-008, EXC-009, EXC-011. They differ on EXC-010 (open by status, absent from the AAR table) and EXC-011 (status worked-around, present in the AAR table as 'removal not completed').
Any answer about what is open states the rule it used, enumerates the members, and discloses this divergence. It is not resolved silently - which set is right is the operator's call.
DIV-002 — evidence coverage across the exceptions
The published AAR says 'Eleven evidence files retained; EXC-011 was reported by the operator with no separate file.' Every sentence there is true, but read together they suggest one uncovered exception. The register holds an evidence field on 6 of the 11 exceptions (EXC-001, EXC-002, EXC-003, EXC-008, EXC-009, EXC-010). Five carry none: EXC-004, EXC-005, EXC-006, EXC-007, EXC-011. And of the 11 files on disk, 3 back a decision or the Airtable obligation rather than an exception (DEC-001, DEC-003, _airtable_obligation), so 8 files cover 6 exceptions.
An evidence answer reports the per-record truth and never infers coverage from the file count. Whether the four unflagged exceptions (EXC-004, EXC-005, EXC-006, EXC-007) should carry evidence is the operator's call, not this layer's.

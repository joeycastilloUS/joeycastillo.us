Install Night 4 — record of events

Kitchen Display System (KDS) installation, overnight 2026-08-05 into 2026-08-06.
West Hollywood (CA.00004 / WEHO) · 4S Ranch (CA.0061) · Santa Monica (CA.0012) · Santa Rosa (CA.0086). Crews from C1 Techs; oversight by Mendocino Farms.

All four stores completed the full cycle — inventory, decommission, install and checkout — and released their technicians. The night’s cadence was recon-first: leads ran site reconnaissance ahead of the planning meeting, the planning meeting was cancelled, and leads called in direct to the operator’s bridge.

The night’s major event was a Konducter platform outage lasting roughly 3.5 hours — first alert at 1:02 AM, all four sites failing identically. The Konducter developers shipped an update at 4:30 AM and the operation was called “SOLID 100% ALL SYSTEMS GO” at that time. C1 KDS Final Reports were received for all four stores.

Ingest health climbed through the night — 2/4 online, then 3/4, then 100% at 12:22 AM. Follow-ups remain (one WEHO NVR to investigate); none blocked go-live.

The major incident — Konducter platform outage

Konducter platform outage — all four sites fail identically (~3.5h)RESOLVED 04:30EXC-400
  • First alert 1:02 AM (WEHO). Over the following hours, testing failed to route to any screen at multiple sites; at Santa Monica testing produced no screen output and no printout, though the Digital Expo printer still printed the alert.
  • WEHO was the lead troubleshooting site with Konducter support on the phone; the operator published an off-spec TOAST change and restarted ingest, but the team was BLOCKED on the platform (2:09a).
  • Konducter requested all sites that could test to test now, knocking down issues FIFO (2:28a). The operator drove a deliberate diagnostic: “I want to know if we all fail the same way.”
  • Confirmed platform-wide: all sites failed the same way. Laura B noted the first alert was 1:02am and it was then 3:04am; “all have failed the same way tonight.”
  • 3:22a — off the phone with Konducter: the dev had found the issue and needed up to one hour; one tech held on site per location for testing.
  • False-positive reporting: to keep process moving, C1 submitted reports flagged as false positives (Konducter still down) at 3:27a; the operator reviewed them.
  • 4:30 AM — Konducter took an update from the developers. Operator: “we are SOLID 100% ALL SYSTEMS GO.”
Evidence — operator “This issue should not be an issue. We will discuss it POST-op. I have a plan to prevent this from ever happening again.” — and at close: “IN THE HEAT OF THE BATTLE.. WE HELD STRONG.. CONGRATULATIONS!”

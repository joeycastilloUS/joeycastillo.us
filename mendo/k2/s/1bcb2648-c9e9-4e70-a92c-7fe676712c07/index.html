Install Night 5 — record of events

Kitchen Display System (KDS) installation, 2026-08-09 into 2026-08-10.
Bellevue · Walnut Creek (CA.0062) · Pacific City (CA.0104) · Gainey Ranch (AZ.0098). Crews from C1 Techs; oversight by Mendocino Farms.

All four stores completed the full cycle and released their technicians. Bellevue ran out of band on a daytime start (techs on site 2:39 PM) and finished 6h 41m wire-to-wire, released 9:11 PM — “SUPER SUPER CLEAN!” The evening three started at 8:30 PM (Pacific City +15, held by the manager for restaurant operations) and flipped all three networks plus Toast config in 45 minutes — a new record.

The night’s major event was the big miss: the Konducter smoke test was not executed at Gainey Ranch. The smoke test was held during Toast config (“HOLD THE SMOKE”), released at 10:14 PM (“SMOKE FREE”) — and Gainey never ran it. A post-flip network fault therefore went undetected until Meraki monitoring flagged the Gainey POS switch offline at 11:11 PM, ~1h 27m after the 9:44 PM flip. The operator carried the diagnosis alone for the rest of the night; the issue was found at 12:31 AM and Gainey was “online and GAINEYING” at 1:51 AM. Walnut Creek and Pacific City finished earlier and waited at standby (~39m and ~57m) before release.

Session closed 2:54 AM — “Unexpected but overcome!” C1 KDS Final Reports were received for all four stores. Countermeasure already set: an 8 PM leads meeting on the operator’s bridge the next day, on the smoke test, to “ensure same-pageness.”

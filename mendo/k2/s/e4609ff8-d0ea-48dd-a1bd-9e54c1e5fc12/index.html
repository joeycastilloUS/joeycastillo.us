Mission Valley — CA.0035

Kitchen went fully downRESOLVED
  • What happened. No orders reached any station. Every screen blank; every device healthy.
  • What was done. The till configuration was examined. The expediter ticket printing mode was set to “only print expediter tickets when the till is in offline mode”. It was changed to always print.
  • Why it did that. Kitchen orders leave the till as expediter tickets to a virtual printer, then to the Ingest box, then to the screens. On that setting the till sends nothing while it is working normally, so the kitchen receives nothing and no equipment reports a fault.
  • Result. Cause identified and corrected on the night.
Steel beam under the counter stopped the drillRESOLVED
  • What happened. The terminal could not be mounted at the position shown; a steel beam ran under the counter.
  • What was done. The approved position photograph from the store that hit the same obstruction the previous night was retrieved, and the technician mounted the terminal to match it.
  • Result. Resolved in minutes. No approval call, no delay.
Ingest box cabled to the wrong socketRESOLVED
  • What happened. The Ingest box was cabled into the second socket on the box itself.
  • What was done. Moved to the first socket.
  • Result. Resolved on the night.

Century City (LACE) — CA.0031

Ingest box was not obtaining a network addressRESOLVED
  • What happened. The Ingest box came up without a network address.
  • What was done. Worked through and corrected during the install.
  • Result. Box is working.
Submission reviewed and releasedRELEASED
  • What was done. The crew’s checkout submission was reviewed against the store instructions and released.
  • Result. Photographs matched the instructions and were quick to compare, because the instructions are now ordered in the same sequence as the record system’s fields.

Davis — CA.0073

Salad screen not registeredOPEN
  • What happened. The Salad screen is not registered and will not run until it is provisioned. It is the only dark station at the store.
  • What was found. In the device list the Salad row is entirely empty — no status, no address, no version, and no activation date. Every other device at the store has one.
  • Open. Needs provisioning. Whether it was never provisioned or lost its registration is not established; the missing activation date points to never provisioned.
Digital Expo screen offlineOPEN
  • What happened. The Digital Expo screen reports offline.
  • What was found. It holds a network address but has never reported a software version. Six of the store’s eight devices are online, including the Ingest box.
  • Open. Needs checking on site.

Encino — CA.0071

Stainless plate not fitted at the Salad stationOPEN
  • What happened. The instructions call for a stainless steel network plate at the Salad station. The crew’s completion photograph shows the original worn plate still in place.
  • What was found. The plate was not delivered to the store.
  • Open. Plate to be supplied and fitted. Identified at checkout, after the crew had left.
Second stainless plate — arrival never confirmedOPEN
  • What happened. The instructions call for a second stainless plate at the Salad Finish station.
  • Open. Whether it arrived at the store was never confirmed, and whether it was fitted is unknown.

Worth acting on

The till setting that took Mission Valley down is not checked anywhereFOR ACTION
  • The expediter ticket printing mode appears in no store instruction, and the on-site test does not cover it. The test confirms the Ingest box answers; it does not confirm a ticket arrives.
  • A store can pass every check and still be kitchen down.
  • Recommended: check the setting at the stores already live, and add it to the instructions and the checkout test.
KDS — the kitchen screen showing orders.
Ingest — the small box that feeds orders to those screens.
Expediter ticket — the kitchen copy of an order, sent from the till.
POS / till — the register system taking customer orders.
Provisioning — registering a screen so the system knows it exists.
Checkout — the photos and form the crew submits at the end of the night.

Kitchen down at Mission Valley — cause found and corrected

Mission Valley — kitchen fully downRESOLVEDCA.0035
  • The store went completely kitchen down — no orders reaching any station.
  • The cause was found in the till system. The printing mode for expediter tickets was set to “only print expediter tickets when the till is in offline mode”.
  • Kitchen orders travel from the till as expediter tickets to a virtual printer, and from there to the Ingest box and on to the screens. With that setting, the till sends nothing while it is working normally. Every station stays blank, and every device still looks healthy, because nothing is broken — nothing is being sent.
  • The setting was corrected. The required state is always print expediter tickets.
  • Also recorded at the time: of the ticket destinations, one virtual destination was enabled; the bread printer, the salad printer and the second virtual destination were not.
Evidence Operator examination of the till configuration during the night, with screenshots of the printing-mode setting.
Why this matters beyond this storeFOR ACTION
  • This setting is named in no store instruction, and the on-site test does not cover it. The test confirms the Ingest box answers; it does not confirm a ticket arrives.
  • A store can pass every check and still be kitchen down. Worth checking the setting at the stores already live, before a lunch service finds it.

Steps required in the field that are written down nowhere

Four steps needed to finish the job appear in no instructionOPEN
  • The till setting that sends kitchen tickets. Named nowhere, and not covered by the on-site test. It took a store down tonight.
  • Which socket on the Ingest box. Every store’s instructions name the socket on the wall switch. None names the socket on the box.
  • The till login code. Still absent after being raised on night 1, when it blocked two stores.
  • What is underneath a counter. No instruction surveys it. The instructions treat the counter as a mounting surface and never inspect it — which is how a steel beam stopped a drill at two stores in two nights.
Note Each is a step needed to finish the job that is not written down. Each came to light through a failure rather than a check.

Recorded at more than one store

Till login code did not work WORKED AROUND EXC-003 · Broadway, Culver City

Testing the screens requires a login to the till system. The code supplied did not work at two stores. Work paused until approximately 01:30, when a temporary elevated account was created to continue.

The login step is not present in the instruction template. 16 of 94 store playbooks have been written to date.

Evidence — Elbert Nghiem “We didn’t include in our playbooks. We should.”

Ingest box did not come online OPEN · WITH SUPPLIER EXC-009

The Ingest box reported offline, at more than one store. Steps taken on site: restart, full power-off, and relocation to a different socket on the network switch. The box remained offline after each.

Referred to Konductor. No resolution at time of writing.

Evidence — tests performed The store network shows the box plugged in and on the same network as the kitchen screens that are working. Tested from a laptop inside the store: no reply from the box, before and after restarts, on more than one socket.

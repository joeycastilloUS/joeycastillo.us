Kaizen — Install Night 3

Nearly every minute lost that night traced to information that already existed — checklists, maps, port labels, criteria, authority — but wasn’t packaged, required, or declared before hands touched the racks. Front-load the ground truth and the rules, and the night executes instead of discovers.

15 improvements · 6 themes · drawn from the 270-message golden transcript · mined by two isolated Opus passes, conformed by Fable. No wins listed — kaizen only.

★ Top 3 — most time saved, least effort
  1. Location-ready packet gate (#1) — recovers the ~40-min blocked start and the late-map stall. Pure process.
  2. Require the playbook photo on every approval (#5) — kills the night’s most-repeated correction with one form field.
  3. Per-team ETAs on the board (#12) — replaces a full night of operator polling with one planning-meeting commitment.

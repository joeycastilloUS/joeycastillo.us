Data hygiene — what the raw fields actually hold

  • Herdserve “Yes”, settled: the RAW Inventory table contains 44 records with literally “Yes” in Herdserve Microtouch Serial Number (80 of 82 records fill the field). The search page's bad() blanks them at index time, so measuring the index finds zero. My earlier “44/77” claim was right about the raw data; the zero-count was right about the index. Both layers were telling the truth about different things. 42 of the 44 are now flagged ⚑.
  • 55 fields hold two serials in one box — 40+ of them Expo Digital Scanner (the two-scanner station). The form has one field for two devices.
  • Free text in serial fields: “no  old salad printer” (01 - 300 Grand) · “no sticker no sn” (48 - Los Feliz) — both pass bad() and get indexed as serials.
  • Homoglyphs and stray punctuation defeat exact match: a Cyrillic Т at Valencia, trailing periods at La Jolla and Walnut Creek. normSN strips spaces/dashes only.
  • The Brea twins diverge on exactly U↔0 and T↔7 — human re-typing fails on the same confusable glyphs OCR does.

The asterisks — on the card, always honest

Even at full coverage, the honest sentence stays: “every ticket judged by 3 Gemini + presiding judge; recall N/N on adjudicated illness; illness among the unflagged bounded <0.1% (95%, n=3,000 human-read control); models <IDs>, run <date>; forward-simulation, not replay.”

Judging every ticket kills the pool-conditional-recall risk from the funnel design — nothing is gated out before the panel. The remaining asterisks are model drift (today’s models on historical text) and a shared blind spot (if all judges miss the same case).

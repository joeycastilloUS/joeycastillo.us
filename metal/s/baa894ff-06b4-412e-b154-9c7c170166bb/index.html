~d — Genesis app: Warrant a Console

Tap-not-type warranting for the operator, air-gap preserved · paired (Opus + Fable5, reconciled across this session) · argus login × kastil-systems/nous purcius-genesis · r930

One line: make the Genesis Android app warrant a console device so the operator logs in by scanning a QR and scanning a QR back — no CLI, no share files, no 128-hex typing — without the app ever touching the network (its core security property). Today the app cannot warrant at all: its native surface exposes mint/recover/resplit/selftest, not warrant. This design adds that op and the two-QR hand-off.
Operator decisions (locked): login target = desktop console · device-pub in = scan a QR · warrant out = QR back to the desktop webcam. Air-gap is non-negotiable.

Need

The operator has the Genesis APK but is stuck at the Argus key-login: his share files are the wrong format (hex text vs the 65-byte binary genesis-cli writes), and there's no easy way to produce the warrant signature (ed25519(root, devicePub||"console")) his browser needs. He asked for the phone to do it — tap, not type. The blocker is concrete: the app has no warrant function, and its no-network manifest forbids the obvious "phone posts the warrant to the server" shortcut.

The constraint that shapes everything: air-gap

purcius-genesis declares no network permission — "this app cannot phone home" (README + manifest). So a server-mediated pairing (phone → bridge → desktop) is forbidden by design. The warrant must leave the phone through a local, offline channel: the screen. Camera-in and QR-out are local operations — they keep the air-gap intact. This is the whole reason the flow is two QRs, not one tap.

The flow (what "easy" looks like)

  1. Desktop Argus login shows the device-pub as a QR (+ the existing hex).
  2. Phone Genesis app → "Warrant a Console" → scan the device-pub QR (camera, local).
  3. App recovers the root from the operator's shares in memory, signs devicePub||"console", wipes the root, renders the 128-hex warrant as a QR (+ Copy).
  4. Desktop login → "Scan warrant" → webcam reads the phone's QR → auto-fills + submits → logged in. Warrant caches (one-time).

Shape — verified slices (each shippable)

Slice 1 — the capability (dependency-free, do first). Add warrant(devpubHex, sharesHex[]) to genesis_jni.c + GenesisNative.kt, and a minimal paste-based "Warrant a Console" screen in MainActivity.kt. This makes the app able to warrant — pure C + Kotlin, no new deps, mirrors go-warrant. New APK, on-device selftest gate.
Slice 2 — QR in. Argus web login renders the device-pub as a QR (tiny pure-JS generator, no external — CSP-safe). App gains camera + a QR decoder (dependency decision: ZXing-Android-Embedded vs CameraX+MLKit vs a vendored pure-C decoder to stay dep-light). CAMERA permission only — still no INTERNET.
Slice 3 — QR out + desktop scan. App renders the warrant as a QR. Argus web login adds a "Scan warrant" webcam reader (getUserMedia + a JS QR decoder) that fills #warrantsave and submits. Fallback to Copy/paste throughout.

Risks / notes

  • Crypto correctness — the JNI must mirror genesis_cli warrant exactly: recover in memory, sign the 39-byte message, secure_zero the root before return. Serial-paired build review mandatory.
  • QR-decode dependency (Slice 2/3) is the one place this app breaks its zero-dep / pure-C ethos — flag for an explicit call before adding a library.
  • Repo hazard — C:\nous is diverged with another session's uncommitted security/mesh_* deletions; build the app in a fresh clone to avoid a collision.
  • Interim to log in TODAY (no app change): fix the share format (genesis-cli writes 65-byte binary share-NN.share, not hex .txt) and run desktop go-warrant.bat <device-pub> → paste. The app flow is the durable easy path; this is the shortcut.

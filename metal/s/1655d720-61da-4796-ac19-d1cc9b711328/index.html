Proving the food-illness screen over real history — three strategies, costs triple-checked.

Recommendation

MED — ~$27 one-time, <$3/month. It runs the exact screen you ship over every one of the 69,400 real guest tickets. LOW is ~$25 cheaper but can’t produce a recall number; HIGH spends ~$110–225 more to rubber-stamp tickets that are already clear. The dollars are a rounding error — the decision is about trust, not budget.

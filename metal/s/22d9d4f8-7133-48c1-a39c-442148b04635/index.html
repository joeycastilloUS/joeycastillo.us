Plan B — the Zendesk flow we're implementing

What actually happens to an inbound email on brand guestrelationstestv2 · every box maps to a DIA unit · 2026-07-23

The whole design in one sentence: the deterministic trigger runs FIRST and pulls illness out of the machine's reach entirely — the AI agent never sees it. Everything the AI does is downstream of a safety gate it cannot override.

The flow

Why the trigger runs before the agent — the design decision

Zendesk's escalation reply sends a message to the customer. If illness escalated through the agent, that message would auto-send to a guest reporting food-borne illness — violating D3's 0 auto-reply on its own acceptance test.

So illness must never reach the agent at all. The keyword trigger fires on ticket create, tags skip_ai_reply, and hands to a human before the AI engages. The agent's escalation reply then only ever fires on the other escalation path — "can't answer" / "customer asked for a human" — where an acknowledgment is fine and D3 isn't implicated.

Two gates, on purpose

Gate 1 — triggerDeterministic. Keyword match, runs every time, cannot be talked out of it. Brittle on paraphrase — that's expected.
Gate 2 — procedureProbabilistic. Catches the paraphrased and oblique cases the keyword list misses ("didn't feel right after", "spent the night in the bathroom"). Obeys as often as a model obeys.

Neither is sufficient alone. The keyword list can't cover language; the model can't be guaranteed. Together they're a floor plus a net — and the gauntlet's 4 paraphrased + 3 oblique + 1 Spanish illness rows exist precisely to measure the gap between them.

What the AI agent is NOT for

No knowledge base, no help-center articles, no use cases, no search rules, no entities, no web forms. This is not a Q&A bot. It reads one email, judges it, and either escalates or composes one reply with a fetched code. Everything else in the AI-agent product is out of scope for this experiment.

Open question for jc — a real DIA gap

The card has exactly two sealed WORDS blocks: illness-procedure and tone. There is no wording for the escalation acknowledgment that Zendesk's escalation reply sends. The DIA needs either a third sealed WORDS block for it, or an explicit rule that illness never reaches the acknowledgment path (which is what this flow implements). Proposed, not written — the card is architecture (Rule 11).

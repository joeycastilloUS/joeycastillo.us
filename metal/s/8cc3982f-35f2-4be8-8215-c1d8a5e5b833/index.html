Defect 2 — 44 Herdserve serials typed “Yes”

Blast radius — 44 of 80 stores (55%). The Herdserve tablet at those stores is unfindable by serial: bad() blanks “Yes” at index time, so search returns nothing. 42 of the 44 carry an operator ⚑ — he is hand-flagging a form defect one row at a time. The 2 unflagged: 86 - Santa Rosa and 01 - 300 Grand.
Root cause — form design. The JotForm question reads as “Herdserve device? …” and techs answer the yes/no, not the serial. A 55% failure rate on one field with a 0% failure rate on its photo is not 44 careless techs — it is one bad prompt.
The fix, where — two layers. (1) Upstream, JotForm: rename the field label to demand the serial, add an input mask/validation (reject “Yes”/“No”, require the 5xxT… shape) — before the Sep 7 wave. (2) One-off data correction, Airtable: every one of the 44 rows has a panel photo read — 37 unanimous 3/3 (paste straight in) and 7 at 2/3 (La Jolla, Vacaville, Bellevue, Rolling Hills, Brentwood, WEHO, Scottsdale — one human glance each). Zero rows are uncorrectable.
Who — operator edits the form; the 44-row correction is an operator-approved batch (we can hand him the exact 44-row serial list from the reads; he pastes or approves an Airtable script he runs himself — no write from us).
Risk + verify — low: correction source is the photo on the same row. Verify: search for 3 corrected serials from 3 stores → each returns its store; raw-table count of “Yes” in the field goes 44 → 0; the 42 workaround flags can then be retired deliberately.
Priority — form fix is P1 (stops ~11 more bad rows in the Sep wave); the batch correction is P4 (valuable, not urgent — the photos aren't going anywhere).

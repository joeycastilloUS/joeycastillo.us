Risk concentration

The template subject (D2, 5 pts) is the item most likely to blow. Everything else in the engine walks proven paths — SwitchPortsAsync, the audit's GetTpl, the Firestore SetAsync pattern at Program.cs:697, cadence's gate. If the shapes don't reconcile, that 5 becomes 5+5.

Contingency is already in the sequencing: it is the last engine item, and the five network subjects ship without it.

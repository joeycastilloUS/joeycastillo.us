The seam that already existed

The .si already bound name resolution to the jacket: resolve_name → bind: http.resolve_name (nous.sm: zbridge_user_name @ comms/wire). The intent was right all along — "the jacket resolves the guest's name." The only wrong thing was a C99 stub that shortcut the bind with a local-part heuristic (chop the email at @, capitalize → "Joey.castillo"). The fix honors the boundary: vendor mapping in the jacket, business-blind plumbing in the runtime.

zendesk-bridge — deterministic env addressing

v3 · NO default · names are asymmetric, rules are symmetric · reconciled Opus + Fable — the navigator overruled the driver's rename · 2026-07-23

Operator directive: there is no default. Not "sandbox," not prod. Every Zendesk-touching call states its target explicitly or it fails. A call whose destination depends on what you forgot to send is not deterministic.

The whole picture

The navigator overruled the driver — and was right

Driver proposed: rename arm A to sandbox-a for symmetry, keeping its HMAC key frozen at "sandbox" via a separate CodeKey field. Navigator rejected it, and the decisive evidence is in our own code:

web/app/index.html:1894 — ENVIRONMENTS.some(e => e.id===v) ? v : "prod". The env selector silently falls back to prod on an unrecognised id. Rename sandbox → sandbox-a and every browser parked on sandbox starts reading production after deploy — no error, no prompt.

Verdict: names are asymmetric, rules are symmetric. Arm A keeps the name sandbox; the name is the HMAC key. No mapping table means nothing to misdeclare per deployment, and no future reader can "fix the inconsistency" and rotate every banked code. Determinism = fewer mappings, not prettier names.

Two gates, not one

Requiring env is necessary but not sufficient. The six sandbox-only routes (alert-create, recovery-send, internal-alert, delete-trigger, sync-object, config-apply) currently make prod impossible — they hard-bind to the sandbox client. Symmetric addressing would quietly turn that into "prod if you name it," so a typo'd env=prod becomes a production ticket write.

Gate 2 fixes it: a per-route slot allow-list. Sandbox routes and promo-claim accept sandbox slots only; env=prod returns 403. That is the biggest new risk the no-default rule introduces, and it is closed by construction.

The enumeration was incomplete — implicit prod is the same sin

The v2 design covered /read, the six gated routes, and promo-claim. It missed roughly nine routes that hit Zendesk on an unnamed prod client: /tickets/{id}/attribution, /templates/rollup, /offscript, /offscript/summary, /nps/*, /zendesk/remediate/plan|apply|rollback, /reply-send, /reply-send/plan.

"No implicit fallback to prod" binds these too. Each must either take a required env, or be explicitly declared prod-pinned in code, in /version, and in the README. Silence is exactly the drift the directive bans.

What breaks — the real caller list

The pipeline callers do not live in argus/src (that is Salesforce-only). They are in metal/src/sdk/nous/action/skill/pirun/pi_run.c — compiled C, so this needs a rebuild and redeploy.

LIVE Zendesk custom actionBody {"ticket_id":"{{ticket.id}}"} — no env. Under strict mode it 400s and the auto-response stalls silently (visible only in Zendesk's action log). Paste: {"ticket_id": "{{ticket.id}}", "env": "sandbox"}. Arm B's own action uses "env": "sandbox-b". Note the route today ignores body env — it must start parsing it.
pi_run.cclaim_code already sends "env":"sandbox" (survives, because we kept the name). internal-alert and recovery-send send no env — break. The ENVQ read macro emits nothing for prod — every prod read breaks. All must always emit env.
Argus web appENV.param() returns "" for prod → the global zdFetch choke point breaks; zdFetchEnv same; config-apply POST sends no env; cross-source query sends none; ENVIRONMENTS needs a sandbox-b entry. zaicFetch is fine — it already pins env=sandbox.
Runbooks / saved callsrunbooks.json, runbook-from-macros.js, bridge-postman.html. Any saved command without env= now 400s — that is the rule working.

Rollout — staged, so the live action never breaks silently

Method

Opus drove v2; Fable independently rejected the driver's slot rename and the address≠codekey split, citing the localStorage prod-fallback at index.html:1894, and caught two blocking gaps the driver missed (the ~9 implicit-prod routes, and prod becoming addressable on sandbox-only routes).

Multi-Language Decode — why #532 false-positived, and the fix jc already designed

Research note (Fable5, grounded — every claim carries file:line). Card: mendo-nps-detractor-triage, unit reduce_feedback. Failure: street-Spanish app complaint escalated as food-borne illness.

1 · Need

Guest #532 (Spanish): "la app está bien fea, me da asco nomás de verla, me enferma lo mal hecha que está" — the APP is ugly; zero food content. The 3-Gemini panel flagged illness (2/3 latched onto "enferma"/"asco"; one said "poorly made food"). The reduction is the cause, not the panel.

2 · What the decoder actually emitted (reproduced, real binary)

Ran the ticket text through the built decoder (src/sdk/nous/action/skill/understand/atoms.exe, compiled from understand.c — the exact engine reduce_atoms.h calls): 
Spanish in  → 19 atoms, EVERY ONE  entity/none:
  la app est bien fea me da asco nom s de verla me enferma lo mal hecha que est
                                                                    (zero structure decoded)
English equivalent in → 22 atoms WITH structure:
  the/structure/specifier  app/property  is/structure/equivalence  really ugly ...
Three concrete losses on the Spanish path:
  • "app" was NOT dropped — it is atom a2. What died is the binding: with "está" unrecognized, no equivalence structure marks app as the subject of fea/enferma. The panel got a flat 19-word bag where asco/enferma/mal/hecha dominate unanchored.
  • Token mangling: is_word_char() (understand.c:106-110) is ASCII-only, so UTF-8 accents break tokens — "está"→"est" (×2), "nomás"→"nom"+"s". The copula is destroyed before lookup could ever match it.
  • Zero reduction: the closed vocabulary (understand.c:14-90, ~90 entries) is 100% English. No Spanish word matches, so nothing is classified as structure — Spanish text passes through UNREDUCED and UNLABELED, the exact opposite of the reducer's value proposition.

4 · Recommendation — (a) detect → dialect → decode, exactly what the a-d-d already says

Do (a). Not (b) raw-to-panel — that concedes the A-B and violates the card's atoms-in-never-prose law (jc order 2026-07-15) for every ticket to cover the non-English minority. Not (c) alone — widening one hardcoded table without detect rebuilds the same wall one language further out and still contradicts the datamap design. The proper fix per what jc actually designed:
  1. UTF-8 tokenizer fix — treat bytes ≥ 0x80 as word chars in is_word_char(). ~5 lines. Kills "est"/"nom s" mangling for every language at once.
  2. Spanish closed-class table — a second Vocab table mapping Spanish structure words to the SAME 28 fns (la/el/las/los→specifier; es/está/son/están/era→equivalence; no/nunca/jamás→negation; de→possessive; en→locative; y→conjunction; o→disjunction; pero→contrast; porque→causal; que→relational; …). Finite, deterministic, ~150-200 entries — the closed classes are the cheap 90% of a dialect.
  3. Deterministic detector — decode against each dialect's table; the dialect with the highest structure-hit-rate wins; below a floor → lang=unknown. The closed vocabulary IS the detector — no new machinery, $0, cannot hallucinate. Emit reduced.lang into the ledger.
  4. Fail-open guard — lang=unknown ⇒ the reducer refuses to pretend: card sends the verbatim feedback to the panel for that ticket (flag-on-doubt already biases safe). Atoms must earn the payload; garble never ships as if it were a reduction.
  5. Bench before arm — add Spanish rows to the corpus, including figurative-clean cases ("me enferma lo mal hecha que está" = clean) and real Spanish illness cases, and re-run the A-B.
Cost: one table + ~40 lines of C + corpus rows; no LLM, no deps, no new skill. The pass-2 property heuristic (understand.c:181-190) already generalizes: "la app está fea" marks app property exactly like "the app is ugly" once está is recognized as equivalence.

Can atoms win cross-language? Yes — the failure was never atomization-as-concept; it was an English lexicon flattening Spanish into an unlabeled bag. With the dialect table, #532 reduces to app/property + está/structure/equivalence + fea/entity — the subject binding the panel needed is exactly what the fix restores. Honest limit: atoms preserve structure, not idiom; a figurative "me enferma" still reaches Gemini as the word — but now anchored to app, which is what flipped 2 of 3 verdicts. Raw is the fallback for unknown languages only, not the plan.

5 · First move

is_word_char() UTF-8 fix + g_vocab_es[] in understand.c, detector in reduce_atoms.h (one serializer, bench and prod move together), Spanish bench rows, re-run pirun_bench. Prod stays disarmed until the A-B is green in both languages.

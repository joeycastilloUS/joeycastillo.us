The full brand picture, from the live API: production has one brand — Mendocino Farms (the real customer surface). Sandbox has five: Brands Testing (default), the two Guest Relations brands above, plus IT Support and Marketing test brands. The A/B runs entirely in sandbox; production is untouched by it.

Same inbound, same sandbox, two brands, two answering systems. The only thing that makes the result trustworthy is that nothing else differs. A stray trigger on one brand, an AI agent left on where it should be off, a macro that exists on one side only — any of those quietly decides the test. Facts is the view that catches them before the numbers come in.

The nuance that matters, stated exactly: the A/B assumption is “AI off on the Argus brand, on for v2.” Three layers, three different answers — and Facts shows all three rather than a single yes/no it cannot honestly give:

  • Account enablement — readable now. Today automatic_answers = false.
  • Per-brand assignment — Admin-Center-only. This one row says “verify in Admin Center.”
  • Actual behaviour — observable per ticket. When a v2 ticket gets a bot answer, the author and tags prove it. Facts can watch, not just ask.

The honest edge is one layer, not the whole fact — and the layer that decides the test can be watched even where it cannot be read.

Every cell is a live count, a plain string, or where the API stops, the words “Admin Center.” No colour that means good or bad — the operator reads the row and judges. The red above is a fact, not a grade: the group auto-assign matches only the production address, so it reaches neither sandbox GR brand — which is why their tickets land with no group.

Almost all of this is already built

The console already has Env Compare and Env Diff — live per-category counts and object-level prod-vs-sandbox diffs across triggers, automations, macros, views, brands, groups, forms, hours — with no grades and a full export engine. They live in navbar modals today.

The build is mostly re-homing and re-pivoting, not inventing: promote that machinery into a Facts tab, and pivot the comparison axis from prod-vs-sandbox to brand-vs-brand within sandbox for the A/B. The prod-vs-sandbox axis stays available — it is still the right tool for parity work. One caveat carried from the source: the Diff modal has a write control (“refresh from prod”). Facts is read-only — it must not inherit that button.

Unknowns

  • Resolved by probe (2026-07-21): account enablement is readable (automatic_answers = false). But the per-brand agent is not surfaced anywhere in the API — no bot user exists, and every AI-agent endpoint probed returns 404. The only per-brand indirect signal is help_center_state: the Argus brand has it enabled, v2 has it disabled, consistent with “no agent built on v2 yet.” Behaviour is unobservable while v2 has zero tickets. So the honest cell stays: enablement readable, assignment Admin-Center, behaviour watchable once traffic arrives.
  • Whether Facts should compare exactly two brands, or list all five with two highlighted. The A/B needs two; parity work may want all.
  • Whether “Grade” keeps its money-tags and RECOMMEND lines, or those are the next thing to question once they are off the facts path.
  • Whether the grade engine moves cleanly or is entangled with the tab badge and comment strip enough to need care (navigator flags ~600 lines including the write path).

When to revisit

The moment the AI-agent state becomes API-readable — that turns the single most important “cannot read” into a real cell and the A/B's core assumption becomes verifiable rather than trusted. Also when webhooks land on the bridge, since a webhook is automation that acts invisibly and a facts view that omits it is quietly incomplete.

Trade to bank in the design

Claim-then-act is at-most-once, not exactly-once end-to-end (Fable). If a sweep wins the claim (created:true) then dies before sending, the count says handled and nothing went out. For a dedup follow-up that is the correct failure — a silent miss beats a double-coupon to the guest, and send is fail:hold so it surfaces. Stated so it's a chosen trade, not a surprise.

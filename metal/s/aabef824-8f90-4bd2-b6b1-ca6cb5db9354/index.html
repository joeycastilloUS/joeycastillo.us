Honest scorecard — today

Argus wins today

  • Illness gate is fail-closed by construction (two-model unanimity + escalate-on-doubt)
  • Guest reply is deterministic, human-authored, $0 — no model writes a guest word
  • One-time coupon claimed atomically in-flow
  • Screen is callable offline — history replayable in principle (Zendesk cannot at all)

Zendesk wins today

  • Multilingual understanding (~150 languages) — Argus's deterministic reader is English-only
  • Live re-evaluation of an active, souring ticket
  • Shipped per-action human-review UI (Copilot) — Argus's is not built
  • Every operational dimension: scale, uptime, support/SLA, compliance, reporting, ecosystem

The frame, stated plainly: Argus is not a Zendesk replacement — it runs on top of Zendesk. Its honest pitch is narrow and sharp: on this one recovery use case, the safety floor is fail-closed, the guest path is deterministic and $0, and history is replayable — three things Zendesk structurally cannot offer. Everything broad and operational still favors Zendesk today, and the Argus edge is sandbox-proven, not production-proven. Biggest gaps to close before this is gold: (1) non-English coverage, (2) run the full-history backtest (pending), (3) ship the per-reply human-review loop, (4) earn production exposure.

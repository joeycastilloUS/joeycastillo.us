  • grab_str full JSON decoding — SKIP; just stop the \u mangle (preserve or minimally decode). A real parser is over-tooling for service-built responses.
  • Retro-editing the legacy .pi path — SKIP. It stays byte-for-byte until the dianoia path retires it.

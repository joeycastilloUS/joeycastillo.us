The endpoint catalog

Every entry is a source-declared template; the caller only fills {params} (URL-encoded, ≤200 chars, no control chars, fails closed) — that is what makes it provably read-only. Each maps 1:1 to the Argus wire form GET /read?source=pulse&id=<id>&<param>=<value>.

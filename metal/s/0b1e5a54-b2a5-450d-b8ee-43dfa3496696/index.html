The Law Audited Against

Language Boundary canon (metal be/docs/conventions.md § Language Boundary; kastil-systems/a-d-d/patterns/PRACTICES.md): C# exists ONLY as a thin jacket over a foreign manufacturer's API — auth, request/response shape translation, retries, pagination. Everything we own is C99 / .pi.

THE METAL PARADIGM — Pure Intent Architecture (be/docs/paradigm.md): the .pi carries the INTENTIONS. Any business logic, mapping, policy, threshold, fixed business value, text the business "says", or routing rule is USER INTENT — stated in the .pi, carried out by Purcius (pirun), never baked into the jacket.

The test: if the C# decides what the system should do, it's a violation. If it decides how to talk to the vendor, it's a jacket.
